One evening. One friend. One plate of egg rolls.

We met through a friend at evening snacks. That ordinary meeting turned into one of the most important introductions of my life.

You were a friend of my friend. We met over evening snacks, and the first conversation I remember involved the egg rolls you made.

The egg roll incident 😂

I didn't even like egg rolls that much, but somehow I still ate two. Looking back, maybe I was just finding an excuse to stay a little longer.

That unforgettable night

One night with you and Alok, talking almost all night, changed the feeling of our friendship. Somewhere in those conversations, a deeper bond quietly grew.

And then… ❤️

Trips, night-outs, jokes, random stories and comfortable silences slowly turned a friendship into the person I wanted beside me for the rest of my life.
